NetShare Player

Your personal media, available across compatible devices.

A Flutter application for browsing, streaming and managing media served by NetShare Server. Android releases are available; iOS availability should not be assumed until officially published.

Verified information and engineering scope.

Technology

Flutter, Dart, cross-platform UI, media playback, device discovery and authenticated file access.

Browse folders, view images and documents, stream audio/video, and manage downloads through a paired server.

Distribution

Android: Google Play and other supported stores. iOS is not currently claimed as released on this portfolio.
